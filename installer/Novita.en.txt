GGCalendar 1.0.2 - What is new
==============================

  - Interface in five languages (it, en, de, es, fr).
  - Search events with keywords.
  - Subscribe to public calendars by URL (read-only).
  - Second time zone in views and events.
  - Create, delete, rename and recolor Google calendars.
  - List of pending alarms, with cancel.
  - Sync queue: see and cancel pending changes.
  - Disconnect an account with a right-click on its name.
