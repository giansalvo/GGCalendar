GGCalendar 1.0.3 - What is new
==============================

  - Choice of the font used in the application;
  - The description of an event shows bold, italic, underline, titles, lists and links;
  - Web addresses and emails in the description are clickable;
  - Improved search (ignores hidden tags);
  - Events can have attachments (https links to files);
  - Sharing of single events through the most common applications.
