GGCalendar 1.0.1 - What's new
=============================

Alarms sync with Google and with GGCalendar Mobile (Android)
------------------------------------------------------------
Up to version 1.0 the alarm (the reminder that rings until you switch it off) existed only
on this computer: after a sync it went back to being a plain notification, and an alarm
set on the phone arrived here as a notification.

From this version alarms travel with the event on Google Calendar, as in
GGCalendar Mobile for Android:

  - an alarm set here also rings on the phone;
  - an alarm set on the phone also rings here;
  - if you remove an alarm on one side, it disappears on the other too.

What to expect
--------------
  - At the first start after the update GGCalendar downloads all the events from Google
    again, once: it will take a little longer. Changes not yet sent are not lost.
  - On the computer, an event cannot have a notification and an alarm with the same number
    of minutes before: in that case the alarm stays.
  - Custom reminder sounds stay on this computer and do not go through Google.
  - The alarm is a private property of your copy of the event: guests do not receive it.

Other changes
-------------
  - The help text in the reminder editor reminds you that alarms are synced.
  - README updated.

Updating
--------
The installer updates the version already present, without deleting your data
(%AppData%\GGCalendar): calendars, settings and Google accounts stay where they are.
