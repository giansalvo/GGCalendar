GGCalendar 1.0.3 - What is new
==============================

  - The description of an event shows bold, italic, underline, line breaks, paragraphs, titles, lists and links as in Google Calendar (read only).
  - Web addresses and emails in the description are clickable; a click asks for confirmation and shows the real address.
  - Search looks at the text you see.
  - The description is limited to 8192 characters.
