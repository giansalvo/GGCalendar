GGCalendar 1.0.5 - What is new
==============================

  - Preview of an event when the mouse rests on it;
  - Revised layout of the event editing window;
  - The Delete key deletes the event whose details card is open;
  - A clear message when Google reports error 403;
  - In Settings you choose the default visibility and status of new events;
  - Reminders appear at the bottom right, in the notification area (the next ones are stacked);
  - Reminders and event times can be set to the minute;
  - Without a network, a "You are offline" message appears instead of the red error;
  - Bug fixes.
