Description

GGCalendar is the ideal way to manage appointments and deadlines efficiently on Windows 11, with a modern interface that supports a light and a dark theme.

Intuitive event management
Plan your days with the day, week, 6-day, month, year and schedule views. Create, move and resize events simply by dragging them, even between different calendars. Manage recurring commitments, local calendars and the import/export of .ics files. A single click opens the quick card to edit, delete, send an email or join Google Meet meetings directly.

Google sync and reminders
Sync one or more Google Calendar accounts in both directions, keeping guests, permissions and Meet links up to date. Smart conflict handling protects your data by saving safety copies. Never miss an appointment thanks to desktop notifications, emails and long sound alarms, which also recover the reminders that fired while the app was closed.

The interface speaks Italian, English, German, Spanish and French: choose the language in Settings, or let GGCalendar follow the language of Windows.

--------

What's new

Alarms sync with Google and with GGCalendar Mobile (Android). An alarm set on the computer also rings on the phone and vice versa; if you remove it on one side it disappears on the other too.

    An event cannot have a notification and an alarm with the same number of minutes before: the alarm stays.
    Custom sounds stay on the computer; guests do not receive your alarms.

------
Product features

 Day, week, 6-day, month, year and schedule views
 Events created, moved and resized by dragging, even between different calendars
 Local calendars and import/export of .ics files
 Desktop notification, email reminders (sent by Google) and a sound alarm that rings until you switch it off, 2 minutes at most
 Two-way sync with one or more Google accounts
 Interface in five languages, chosen in Settings

------

GGCalendar organises appointments and deadlines on Windows 11 with a light/dark theme. It offers several views, drag-and-drop, .ics handling and two-way sync with Google Calendar. It includes desktop notifications, email and alarms that recover missed reminders.
