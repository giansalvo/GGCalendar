Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026 · GGCalendar v1 for Windows and GGCalendar Mobile for Android · Translation of the Italian original, which is the reference text

This policy describes how GGCalendar for Windows and GGCalendar Mobile for Android (together, "the App") and the related website process personal data, under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), and how the App uses the data received from Google APIs.

1. Data controller and scope

Giansalvo Gusinu, Sassari (Italy). Contact: write to the controller..

The controller processes personal data only for the website (section 9) and for email communications (section 10). The App works entirely on your device (computer or phone): the author does not receive, see or keep your events or any other data entered or synced in the App. If you connect a Google account, your calendar data is processed by Google LLC as an independent controller, under Google's Privacy Policy..

2. The App in brief

  • it does not require creating an account: without connecting Google it works only locally;
  • it does not send data to servers of the author: the author does not run any server for the App;
  • it communicates over the network only with Google services, and only if you connect an account (section 4);
  • it contains no advertising or profiling tools and does not collect usage or telemetry data;
  • it does not sell or give data to anyone.

3. Data saved on your device

3.1 Windows

The App saves its data in the %AppData%\GGCalendar folder of your Windows user:

  • calendar.db: the calendars and events (title, dates, place, description, guests, reminders, colours), both of local calendars and of the copy of the synced Google calendars, plus the changes waiting to be sent to Google;
  • settings.json: the settings of the App;
  • reminders.json: the reminders already shown or postponed;
  • google-accounts.json: the email address of the connected Google accounts and the related access token, encrypted with Windows data protection (DPAPI) and usable only by your Windows user.

The events database is not encrypted by the App and is protected by access to your Windows account: we recommend protecting the account with a password and, if possible, turning on disk encryption (BitLocker). Backup, retention and deletion of these files are under your control (section 8).

3.2 Android

The App saves its data in the private storage that Android reserves for each app, not accessible to other apps:

  • the database of calendars and events (same contents described for Windows), with the changes waiting to be sent to Google;
  • the settings of the App and the state of the reminders already shown or postponed;
  • the email address of the connected Google accounts.

On Android the App does not keep any access token: when it has to communicate with Google, it asks Google Play Services for one each time, the system component that manages the Google accounts of the phone. The App's data is excluded from Android's automatic backup, so it is not copied to your Google backup space. We recommend protecting the phone with a screen lock.

4. Sync with Google Calendar (optional)

If you connect a Google account, sign-in takes place through Google's sign-in page in the browser (Windows) or through the account choice and the authorisation screen of Google Play Services (Android), using the OAuth 2.0 protocol: the App neither sees nor keeps your Google password.

4.1 Google data the App accesses

The App asks for the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar permission (see, edit, share and permanently delete all the calendars you can access using Google Calendar), needed for two-way sync. With this permission the App reads and writes:

  • the list of your calendars (name, colour, default reminders, access level);
  • the events of those calendars: title, dates and times, place, description, recurrence, guests and their email addresses, reminders, availability, visibility, Google Meet link and guest permissions;
  • the email address of the account, to identify it in the App.

4.2 How it is used

This data is used only to show you your calendars in the App, to create, edit and delete events at your request and to show you reminders on your device. It is not used for advertising, profiling or analytics, nor to train artificial intelligence models, and it is not read by people.

4.3 Retention

A copy of the synced data is kept only on your device, as indicated in section 3, until you disconnect the account or delete the App's data. The original data stays in your Google account and is managed by Google.

4.4 Sharing

The data travels only between your device and Google's servers, over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), without going through servers of the author or of third parties, and is not transferred to anyone else. Through the App you can, however, ask Google to contact other people:

  • if you add guests to an event with the "send invitations" option on, Google sends them the invitation, update or cancellation emails;
  • if you add a video call, Google creates the Google Meet link;
  • "email" reminders are sent by Google to your address.

4.5 Limited Use

GGCalendar's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements., inclusi i requisiti di Limited Use (utilizzo limitato).

4.6 Revoking access

You can disconnect an account at any time from Settings → Google accounts: the App revokes its own authorisation with Google and deletes from the device the token (on Windows) and the copy of that account's calendars. If you are offline at that moment, the account is still removed from the device and you can revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting does not delete events from your Google account.. Lo scollegamento non cancella gli eventi dal tuo account Google.

5. Other people's data

Events may contain data of other people, for example the email addresses of guests. This data is managed by you, on your device and in your Google account; the author has no access to it. Please enter third-party data only when necessary.

6. Local artificial intelligence models (Ollama, Windows only)

In the Windows version, in the settings you can enter the address of an Ollama installation (default: your own computer). In version 1 the App only asks that address for the list of available models and does not send events or other calendar data. If you enter the address of another computer, the request reaches that computer.

7. Import, export, sharing and printing

The .ics files you import or export are read and written only on your device, in the places you choose. On Android, when you share or print an event, the content is passed to the app or printer you choose (for example a mail or messaging app), which handles it under its own rules.

7.1 Permissions requested on Android

  • Internet: to communicate with Google and, for the update check, with GitHub;
  • Notifications, alarms and timers, full screen, phone start-up: to show reminders and alarms at the right time, even with the screen locked and after a restart;
  • Contacts (optional): to suggest the email addresses of guests while you type. Contacts are read only on the phone and are not sent to anyone, except the addresses you choose to add as guests;
  • App installation: to install the App's updates downloaded from GitHub, only when you confirm.

The sounds of the reminders and the .ics files are chosen through Android's file picker: the App accesses only the files you indicate.

8. Deleting the App's data

To delete from the device the data of a single Google account, disconnect it (section 4.6).

Android: uninstalling the App deletes all its data from the phone. You can also delete it without uninstalling, from Settings → Apps → GGCalendar → Storage → Clear data.

Windows: uninstalling the App does not delete the data, so as not to lose it in case of reinstallation or update: to delete it completely, after uninstalling delete the %AppData%\GGCalendar folder (paste the path in the address bar of File Explorer). If you had connected Google accounts without disconnecting them, also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions..

9. Data processed by the website

The pages of this site use no cookies or analytics or third-party tools. The site is hosted on Altervista (altervista.org), which records in the server logs technical browsing data (for example IP address, date and time, requested page) for security and the correct operation of the service. Altervista may also use its own or third-party cookies, for example for advertising on freely hosted sites: this processing is managed by Altervista, which describes it in its own policy and in the cookie banner shown on the first visit.), che registra nei log del server dati tecnici di navigazione (ad esempio indirizzo IP, data e ora, pagina richiesta) per finalità di sicurezza e corretto funzionamento del servizio. Altervista può inoltre utilizzare cookie propri o di terze parti, ad esempio per la pubblicità sui siti ospitati gratuitamente: questi trattamenti sono gestiti da Altervista, che li descrive nella propria informativa e nel banner dei cookie mostrato alla prima visita.

The installation files of the App (for Windows and for Android) are downloaded from GitHub (GitHub, Inc., United States), which processes the technical data of the download, such as the IP address, under its own privacy statement. The Android App periodically checks GitHub for a new version: the request contains no personal data, but GitHub receives the phone's IP address.. L'App per Android controlla periodicamente su GitHub se è disponibile una nuova versione: la richiesta non contiene dati personali, ma GitHub riceve l'indirizzo IP del telefono.

10. Email communications

If you write to the contact address, I will use your email address and the content of the message only to reply to you. Messages are kept for the time needed to handle the request and in any case no longer than 24 months after the last exchange, and are not given to third parties. The contact address is a Gmail account: the messages are therefore kept on Google's servers.

11. Legal basis

The processing of contact data is based on the need to respond to your request and on the legitimate interest in replying to the communications received (art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). Server logs are based on the legitimate interest in the security of the site (point f). For the data in the App the author carries out no processing (section 1).

12. Transfer of data outside the European Union

Google (for sync and for the contact email) and GitHub (for the download and the update check) may process data in the United States. Both companies adhere to the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and adopt the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

13. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, rectify it, erase it, restrict its processing, object and, where applicable, obtain its portability. You can also lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority (garanteprivacy.it). To exercise your rights write to the contact indicated above. For the data of your Google account you can also use Google's tools (myaccount.google.com).). Per esercitare i tuoi diritti scrivi al contatto indicato sopra. Per i dati del tuo account Google puoi usare anche gli strumenti di Google (myaccount.google.com).

14. Minors

The author does not knowingly collect data of minors through the App or the site.

15. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated, for example in case of new features in future versions of the App. The date of the last update is shown at the top; any significant changes will be announced on the site.

16. Contact

For any question about this policy: write to the author..
