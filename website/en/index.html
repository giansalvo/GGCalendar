Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

If you have ever used an online calendar, you will feel at home right away: the same logic, the same clarity, in a light app designed for Windows 11.

Day, week and month views

Switch with one click from the monthly overview to the detail of the day, with the current day always highlighted.

Events in a few seconds

Create an event with a title, date, time, place and notes. Edit or move it when plans change.

Reminders

Get a notification before the event, so you never miss deadlines, classes and appointments again. For important reminders there is the alarm, which rings until you switch it off.

Recurring events

Repeat a commitment every day, week, month or year without having to enter it again each time.

Search events

Look for a word in the title, description, location and guests, in one or more calendars, with optional dates and words to leave out.

Google Calendar sync

Connect one or more Google accounts, if you like: calendars, events and reminders stay aligned in both directions. Alarms too: those set here also ring on GGCalendar Mobile for Android, and vice versa.

Your data, under control

No account required and no server run by the author: your data stays on your computer or, if you connect Google, travels only between the PC and your Google account. Details in the privacy policy.
